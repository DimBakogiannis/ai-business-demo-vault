Mozilla's research team attributes the breakthrough both to the Mythos model's increased capability and to improved techniques for harnessing AI systems, including agentic systems that can assess their own work and filter false positives.

## Security Researcher Demonstrates Alarming Vulnerabilities in Yarbo Robot Lawn Mowers

A chilling demonstration of IoT security vulnerabilities played out when researcher Andreas Makris proved he could remotely hijack thousands of Yarbo robot lawn mowers from anywhere in the world—including taking control of a 200-pound machine while it operated dangerously close to a prone person on the ground. As reported by The Verge, Makris discovered that the $5,000 yard robots contain multiple critical security flaws that would allow an attacker to command any or all of the approximately 11,000 Yarbo devices globally.

The vulnerabilities are staggering in scope. Makris demonstrated that he could not only remotely control individual machines but could access what appears to be every Yarbo robot in operation. He showed a map containing approximately 5,400 devices in the United States and Europe alone. Most alarmingly, the robots include built-in Linux operating systems with hardcoded, unchangeable root passwords, giving attackers complete administrative access. The security researcher explained: "I can do whatever I want with all the bots. It's completely unsecured."

The implications extend far beyond inconvenience. Because these machines have rotating blades for lawn mowing, snow removal, and trimming, hackers could theoretically activate the blades remotely or override safety mechanisms. Makris demonstrated that even pressing the physical emergency stop button provides no protection if a remote attacker sends an unlock command. Beyond direct physical danger, compromised devices could be reprogrammed into botnets, weaponized to probe home networks, or used for other malicious purposes. Founded in 2015, Yarbo manufactures modular yard robots with interchangeable attachments, meaning the security flaws likely affect all its variants, from snowblowers to leaf blowers.

**Industry Impact:** This incident underscores the persistent gap between the pace of IoT device deployment and the maturity of IoT security practices.
