# Apple Intelligence Opens to Third-Party Developers

> [!summary] TL;DR
> [[Apple]] released the Apple Intelligence SDK, letting third-party iOS apps tap into the on-device foundation model and Private Cloud Compute. The move pushes [[Generative AI]] further into the consumer mainstream.

## Key Concepts
[[Apple]], [[Generative AI]], [[Multimodal AI]], [[Large Language Models]], [[AI in Business]], [[AI Safety]]

## Summary
The new SDK exposes a writing tools API, an image playground API, and a system-level "App Intents" interface that lets apps participate in Siri-driven [[AI Agents|agentic]] workflows. [[Apple]] is positioning the platform as a privacy-preserving alternative to cloud-only services from [[OpenAI]] and [[Google]], with most inference running locally on Apple Silicon.

For sensitive queries the system falls back to Private Cloud Compute, which Apple says is independently auditable.

## Why It Matters
On-device [[Multimodal AI]] removes a major barrier for [[AI in Business]] use cases involving regulated data — medical, legal, financial — where data residency makes cloud LLMs infeasible. It also intensifies competition for developer mindshare against the [[OpenAI]]/[[Microsoft]] axis.
