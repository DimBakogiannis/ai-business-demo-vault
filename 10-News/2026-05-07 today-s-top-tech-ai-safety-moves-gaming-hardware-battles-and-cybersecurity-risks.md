## OpenAI Expands ChatGPT Safety Features With Trusted Contact Alerts

**OpenAI is launching a new optional safety feature called "Trusted Contact" that allows adult ChatGPT users to designate emergency contacts who will be notified if the AI detects signs of self-harm or suicide concerns.** The feature expands existing teenage safety options to adults 18 and older globally, with a slightly higher threshold of 19+ in South Korea. This represents a significant step in AI safety as companies increasingly grapple with their responsibility toward vulnerable users.

The Trusted Contact system operates through a layered approach designed to balance privacy with intervention. Users can assign contact details for fellow adults through their ChatGPT account settings, and the designated contact must accept the invitation within seven days. The system is intentionally limited—it does not share chat transcripts or conversation details with the Trusted Contact. Instead, when OpenAI's automated detection systems identify concerning language patterns around self-harm or suicide, the platform will encourage the user to reach out to their contact and inform them that notification may occur. A specially trained review team then evaluates the situation, and if serious safety concerns are confirmed, the Trusted Contact receives a brief notification via email, text message, or in-app alert.

This new feature builds directly on lessons learned from tragedy. In 2024, OpenAI introduced emergency contact features for teenage users following the death of a 16-year-old who had spent months confiding in ChatGPT before taking his own life. Meta has similarly introduced features that alert parents when their children repeatedly search for self-harm content on Instagram. OpenAI emphasizes that the Trusted Contact feature is designed around "expert-validated" principles—the simple concept that connecting someone in crisis with a trusted person can make a meaningful difference. Users retain full control, able to modify or remove their chosen contact at any time, while Trusted Contacts can also opt out of the arrangement.

The rollout reflects broader industry trends around AI accountability. As conversational AI systems become more deeply integrated into daily life—especially for vulnerable populations—companies face mounting pressure to implement safeguards that go beyond simple content filtering. The feature represents OpenAI's attempt to position itself as a responsible actor in mental health support, though critics argue that AI systems should never be a substitute for professional mental health care. The feature's effectiveness will ultimately depend on user adoption rates and whether the review team can accurately distinguish genuine crises from routine discussions about difficult topics.

## Valve Combats Steam Controller Scalpers With Reservation Queue System

Valve explicitly acknowledged this in its announcement, stating: "While we were happy to see such a high level of interest, the experience for a lot of you trying to buy it was incredibly frustrating." The reservation system works by saving purchasers' places in line based on when they reserved the controller. Once inventory becomes available, Valve will send order emails in the order that reservations were made. Customers will have 72 hours to complete their purchase and are limited to buying one controller. This last requirement serves as a basic anti-scalper measure, preventing brand-new accounts from hoarding stock. For now, customers who already purchased a Steam Controller cannot reserve another, though Valve indicated this policy may evolve.

Fulfillment begins next week in the US and Canada, with rollouts in the UK, EU, and Australia following in subsequent weeks. This staggered approach allows Valve to manage manufacturing and logistics while monitoring the reservations queue. The implementation mirrors Valve's approach to the original Steam Deck launch, though notably that reservation queue took several months to clear. The Steam Controller's rapid resale prices demonstrate enduring demand for specialized gaming hardware, and Valve's reservation strategy suggests the company has learned from past launches about the importance of structured inventory distribution. However, determined scalpers may still find workarounds, particularly once controllers begin shipping and secondary market demand peaks.
