# Perplexity Raises $1.5B at $18B Valuation

> [!summary] TL;DR
> [[Perplexity]] closed a $1.5B Series F led by SoftBank at an $18B valuation. The company plans to expand its enterprise tier and ship a native browser by end of year.

## Summary
The funding round confirms [[Perplexity]] as the leading independent contender in AI-native search, despite competitive pressure from [[Google]]'s AI Overviews and [[OpenAI]]'s ChatGPT Search. Enterprise revenue reportedly tripled in the last twelve months on the back of contracts with consulting firms and financial services.

The planned browser would put [[Perplexity]]'s search interface in front of users by default, bypassing the need to compete on existing search distribution.

## Why It Matters
A standalone AI-native browser is a serious distribution play — and a credible threat to the long-running Google search monopoly. It also points to where vertical [[AI in Business]] tools are heading: bundling [[AI Agents]] directly into the workflow rather than as add-ons.
