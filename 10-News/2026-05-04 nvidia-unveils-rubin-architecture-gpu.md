# Nvidia Unveils Rubin Architecture GPUs at GTC

> [!summary] TL;DR
> [[Nvidia]] announced the Rubin GPU architecture, claiming a 3.3× training throughput improvement over Blackwell. The flagship R200 ships in Q3 2026 with first allocation going to [[Microsoft]], [[Amazon]], [[Google]], and [[Meta]].

## Summary
The Rubin architecture moves to a 3nm process and introduces native FP4 and FP6 precision modes targeted at inference of [[Large Language Models]]. [[Nvidia]] also announced a new Spectrum-7 interconnect and the NVL576 rack-scale system, which the company claims can train a trillion-parameter model in under three weeks.

Hyperscaler pre-orders reportedly account for over 80% of 2026 production capacity.

## Why It Matters
Compute scarcity remains the single biggest bottleneck for [[AI Infrastructure]] expansion. Rubin's efficiency gains are large enough that some training jobs that were uneconomical on Blackwell become viable. For [[Enterprise AI]] customers without hyperscaler relationships, however, GPU access will likely tighten further before it loosens.
