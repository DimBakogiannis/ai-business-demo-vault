# Meta Doubles Down on Open-Source AI Strategy

> [!summary] TL;DR
> [[Meta]] CEO [[Mark Zuckerberg]] reaffirmed the company's commitment to [[Open Source AI]], announcing a $10B research budget increase and the formation of a Llama Foundation alongside Chief AI Scientist [[Yann LeCun]].

## Key Concepts
[[Meta]], [[Mark Zuckerberg]], [[Yann LeCun]], [[Llama]], [[Open Source AI]], [[Hugging Face]], [[Mistral]], [[AI Regulation]]

## Summary
The Llama Foundation will hold the [[Llama]] trademark and license, decoupling it from [[Meta]]'s corporate roadmap. [[Mark Zuckerberg]] framed the move as a defensive play against the closed-API dominance of [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]], echoing arguments [[Yann LeCun]] has made publicly for over a year.

[[Mistral]] CEO [[Arthur Mensch]] welcomed the announcement, saying it strengthens the case for European [[AI Regulation]] to formally recognize open weights as a distinct regulatory category.

## Why It Matters
A foundation-governed [[Llama]] insulates the model family from any future commercial pressure to close it down — which has been a recurring fear in the [[Open Source AI]] community. Combined with [[Mistral]]'s Apache-licensed releases, the open ecosystem now has institutional infrastructure that can outlast any single company's strategy shifts.
