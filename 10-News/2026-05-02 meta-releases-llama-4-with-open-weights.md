# Meta Releases Llama 4 With Open Weights

> [!summary] TL;DR
> [[Meta]] released [[Llama]] 4 in three sizes (8B, 70B, 405B) under a commercially permissive license. Weights are available on [[Hugging Face]] and through major cloud providers. Benchmarks place the 405B variant near closed-source frontier models.

## Key Concepts
[[Meta]], [[Llama]], [[Open Source AI]], [[Large Language Models]], [[Hugging Face]], [[Mistral]], [[AI Infrastructure]]

## Summary
Llama 4 introduces Mixture-of-Experts at all three sizes, native 1M-token context, and improved multilingual coverage across 40+ languages. [[Meta]]'s license remains the same as Llama 3.1: commercial use is permitted up to 700M monthly active users, after which a separate license is required.

The 70B variant is positioned as the workhorse for [[Enterprise AI]] deployments where data residency or fine-tuning matters more than raw frontier performance.

## Why It Matters
Open-weight models at frontier-adjacent quality keep [[OpenAI]] and [[Anthropic]] honest on pricing and lock-in concerns. For organizations where [[AI Safety]] reviews or compliance constraints make closed APIs infeasible, [[Llama]] 4 substantially expands what's deployable in-house.
